RENDER SETTINGS FOR THIS PROJECT

Frontend service (mrkzone-fixed-1):
  VITE_API_URL=https://mrkzone-fixed.onrender.com/api

Backend service (mrkzone-fixed):
  FRONTEND_URL=https://mrkzone-fixed-1.onrender.com
  UPLOAD_DIR=/var/data/uploads

Attach a Render Persistent Disk to the BACKEND service with mount path:
  /var/data

The backend saves product files under /var/data/uploads/products and serves
them at /uploads/products/<filename>. Do not append /bakend or /frnted to
the service URLs; those words are not part of the service domains.

After setting the environment variables and disk, redeploy both services.
